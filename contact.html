FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our onboarding, fee structure, and security protocols.

What is the minimum portfolio size required to work with Stackly?

Our private wealth advisory tier starts at $500,000 in liquid investable assets. Corporate treasury and institutional plans begin at $2M and $10M respectively.

How are advisory fees structured?

We operate on a transparent tier-based Assets Under Management (AUM) fee model, typically ranging from 0.40% to 0.85% annually, with zero hidden trade commissions.

Which regulatory bodies oversee Stackly?

Stackly is registered with the SEC in the United States, FCA in the UK, and MAS in Singapore, adhering strictly to global fiduciary and compliance standards.

Can Stackly integrate with our existing custodian banks?

Yes. We integrate seamlessly with major global custodian institutions including Charles Schwab, Fidelity, BNY Mellon, and UBS via secure API connectivity.
